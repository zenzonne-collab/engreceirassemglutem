My Story

Why I started baking gluten-free

I'm Matheus. I started making gluten-free bread for a health reason — I was feeling bloated, tired, and struggling to lose weight, and I realized regular bread was part of the problem.

But I didn't want to give up bread completely. It's part of my routine, part of my life. So I started testing gluten-free recipes at home, and honestly, at first it was frustrating — the results were dry, dense, or loaded with ingredients that didn't help at all.

😩 Dry & crumbly 🧱 Too dense 😑 Tastes like cardboard 🛒 Weird ingredients

Over time, I learned the right flour combinations and ratios that actually make a soft, light loaf — the kind that helps reduce bloating and keeps hunger in check, instead of working against you.

Today I've put everything I learned into one simple, straightforward collection, so anyone can bake gluten-free bread that works — even with zero experience.
